System design

How a Twin delivers work

Product layer

Personal AI, introductions and service orders share the same Twin identity.

Five capabilities

The counterpart interprets your experience. Research checks sources. Judgment compares actions. Simulation tests a choice under stated conditions. Adjudication checks delivery and whether observed results support the original judgment. Disputes can go to a person.

Models, data and accounts

Permissions govern each use of a record. Corrections, accepted deliveries and later real outcomes provide distinct evidence. A service ledger connects the quote, actual costs, acceptance and income.

Compute and storage

Runtime, scheduling, backups and recovery consume resources. A service agreement must specify what pauses at the budget limit and what remains available.
